Frequently asked questions

Is Musclivo free?

You can try every feature free for 3 days with no card. After that you can continue with a monthly membership that includes personal coaching.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Musclivo works straight in the browser on your phone or computer. You can also install it on your home screen so it opens like a normal app and sends reminders.

Can I use Musclivo to lose weight?

Yes. The app calculates your daily calorie target from your age, height, weight and activity level, and helps you keep a moderate calorie deficit with enough protein.

Is the app available in Arabic?

Yes. Musclivo is available in Arabic, English, Swedish and nine other languages, and you can switch language at any time.

Is my health data safe?

Your data is stored securely and is only used to provide the service. Read more in our privacy policy.
